Waveband is a damage meter that sits on top of Wuthering Waves.

It is passive: it reads the game's own log file and looks at the screen. It never opens, reads or changes the game process, and it never presses keys for you.

It is an unofficial fan tool, not made or endorsed by Kuro Games, and Kuro Games has not approved it. Whether to run it is your own call.

Waveband installs for your Windows account only, with no administrator rights, and asks GitHub for a newer version when it starts (you can switch that off in its Settings). It sends nothing about you or your game.
